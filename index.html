Moderators only.

A moderator is a member with extra reading rights, not a superuser. You can see reports, hide a listing and suspend an account. You cannot touch a wallet or the ledger — nobody can, and that is the point.

Drifted wallets

System balance is the sum of every ledger entry ever written, in minutes. Anything but zero means time was made from nothing. Drifted wallets are wallets whose cached balance disagrees with their ledger; both should stay at zero for ever.
